acne::Use BHA or niacinamide; avoid heavy oils.
oily skin::Use gel cleansers, niacinamide, BHA, and oil-free moisturizers.
dry skin::Use cream cleansers, ceramides, and hyaluronic acid; avoid foaming cleansers.
sensitive skin::Use fragrance-free cleanser and moisturizer; avoid strong acids and retinol.
hyperpigmentation::Use niacinamide or azelaic acid; avoid strong vitamin C if sensitive.
pimples::Use BHA or salicylic acid; avoid heavy oils.
blackheads::Use BHA; avoid pore-clogging products.
whiteheads::Use gentle exfoliation and BHA; avoid heavy creams.
dark spots::Use niacinamide or azelaic acid; avoid harsh scrubs.
redness::Use soothing ingredients like centella or niacinamide; avoid strong exfoliants.
large pores::Use niacinamide and BHA; avoid thick oils.
sun damage::Use SPF 30+ daily; avoid unprotected sun exposure.
dehydrated skin::Use hyaluronic acid and light moisturizers; avoid alcohol-heavy products.
combination skin::Use gel cleansers; moisturize dry areas; avoid heavy oils in T-zone.
